Getting started

Quick Start

First time? Follow these steps to be up and running in under 10 minutes.

1

Read the Factor Definitions sheet

Open the Excel and navigate to Factor Definitions. Read it fully before scoring — ensures your scores are comparable to other PMs'.

2

Open the Risk Scorecard sheet

Find your product row. Blue cells are yours to fill — enter a score 1–10 for each of the five factors.

3

Check the auto-calculated Risk Score

The grey Risk Score and Risk Level columns calculate automatically. Do not edit grey cells directly.

4

Update Portfolio Summary sheet

Copy your Risk Score to column D. Update Risk Level (C), Trend (E), Status (F), and Key Concern (G) for your product.

5

Update the Risk Register if anything changed

Update threat descriptions, counter-actions, and action owners. Add new risk rows if new threats emerged.

6

Save and share the Excel file

Save and share with the Portfolio Strategy lead by COB Friday.

7

Upload to the dashboard (Portfolio lead)

Click Portfolio View in the sidebar → click Upload Excel file…. All charts and cards refresh immediately. Click Export PDF for the leadership report.

⚠ Important

Only enter values in blue input cells (columns C–G) in the Risk Scorecard. Do not edit grey calculated cells.

Scoring methodology

The Formula

Every product's Risk Score is calculated from five weighted factors, each scored 1–10 by the responsible Product Manager.

Risk Score =
  (Imminence × 0.30) +
  (Business Impact × 0.25) +
  (Competitive Pressure × 0.20) +
  (Market Visibility Gap × 0.15) +
  (Churn Signal × 0.10)

Risk level thresholds

CRITICAL
Score ≥ 8.5 — Immediate escalation. Executive attention and dedicated resource allocation.
HIGH
Score 6.5–8.4 — Under active review. Counter-actions must be in progress with named owners.
MEDIUM
Score 4.5–6.4 — Monitored. Documented risks with a clear watch plan.
LOW
Score below 4.5 — No material risk. Review quarterly.

Why these weights?

FactorWeightRationale
Threat Imminence30%A threat happening now demands immediate resource; a theoretical future threat does not.
Business Impact25%Severity matters — a threat to 70% of ARR outweighs a niche displacement even at equal imminence.
Competitive Pressure20%Named, funded competitors with active sales motions are more dangerous than market trends.
Market Visibility Gap15%Analyst invisibility prevents new business but doesn't directly accelerate churn.
Churn Signal10%A lagging indicator — by the time it scores high, other factors have already escalated.

✅ Scoring rule

Always score based on current evidence you can cite — a customer quote, analyst report, competitive win/loss record, or renewal data.

Factor 1 — Threat Imminence (30%)

Definition: How soon and concretely is the threat materialising? Score based on confirmed evidence, not theoretical possibility.

What to assess: Is a competitor actively selling against this product today? Is there a regulatory deadline or technology shift with a fixed date? Has the threat caused measurable commercial damage?

Timeframe guide: Under 6 months → high imminence · 6–18 months → medium · Over 18 months → low

Scoring methodology

Calibration Guide

Consistent scoring across PMs is critical. Use this guide to anchor your scores.

Common calibration mistakes

MistakeWhat it looks likeHow to fix it
Grade inflationEvery factor scored 8–10 "to be safe"Reserve 9–10 for confirmed, in-market threats only. No evidence = score 1–3.
Grade deflationScores kept low to avoid scrutinyIf customers are asking about alternatives, Churn Signal is 6+. Competitor actively selling = Imminence 8+.
Anchoring to last weekCopying last week's scores without reassessingStart fresh. Ask: "What changed this week that would move any factor?"
Scoring the company, not the productHigh scores because "general perception problem"Each factor must relate to this product's specific threat evidence.

Support

FAQ

Can I add a new product row to the Excel?

Yes — add a new row in both Risk Scorecard and Portfolio Summary (keep within rows 5–16). Contact the Portfolio Strategy lead if you need more than 12 products.

What if I disagree with the scoring weights?

Weights are set by portfolio leadership for portfolio-wide comparability. Raise disagreements in the weekly review for collective discussion.

The dashboard doesn't show my updated scores after upload

Check: (1) you saved before uploading; (2) product name in column A matches the dashboard; (3) Risk Level in column C is exactly CRITICAL, HIGH, MEDIUM, or LOW from the dropdown.

Can multiple PMs edit the Excel at the same time?

Not in the current setup. Use a shared file on SharePoint/OneDrive with one PM editing at a time, or each PM sends their rows to the Portfolio lead who consolidates.

Where is the data stored?

All data lives in the Excel file. The dashboard reads it on upload and holds it in browser memory — nothing is sent to any server. Action statuses and history are in browser local storage.

How do I reset action status tracking?

Go to Upload History in the sidebar and click "Clear history". Or clear browser local storage via Settings → Privacy → Clear browsing data.
